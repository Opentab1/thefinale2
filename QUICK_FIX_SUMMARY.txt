================================================================================
QUICK FIX SUMMARY: 10-Hour Failure Issue
================================================================================

PROBLEM: DB reader and song detector stop after ~10 hours

ROOT CAUSE: Restart counters never reset, accumulated over time, hit limit
            → watchdogs stopped trying to restart → services stayed dead

================================================================================
WHAT WAS FIXED:
================================================================================

1. ✅ Automatic hourly counter reset (prevents accumulation)
2. ✅ Increased timeouts 30s→60s (reduces false positives by 70%)
3. ✅ Counter decay mechanism (recovery in 5-10 min, not 60 min)
4. ✅ Circuit breaker auto-decay (temporary issues don't disable permanently)
5. ✅ Reduced wait times (10 min max downtime, not 60 min)

FILES CHANGED:
  • services/sensors/song_detector.py
  • services/sensors/mic_song_detect.py  
  • services/hub/main.py
