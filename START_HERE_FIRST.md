## You Want: Zero Coding, Just a Working System

## The ONE Command You Need:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Opentab1/thefinale2/main/install.sh | sudo bash
```

## What Happens:

### Step 1: Installation (15-20 minutes - automatic)
- Your Pi downloads and installs everything
- You can walk away, it's fully automatic
- When done, it will say "Rebooting in 10 seconds..."

### Step 2: First Reboot (1 minute - automatic)
- Pi reboots on its own
- Browser opens automatically to the setup wizard
- URL: `http://localhost:9090`

### Step 3: Setup Wizard (2 minutes - YOU click buttons)
This is the ONLY part where you do something:

**Screen 1: Venue Setup**
- Enter your venue name (or keep default)
- Select your timezone
- Click "Next"

**Screen 2: Hardware Check**  
- Just shows what sensors it found
- All automatic, nothing to do
- Click "Next"

**Screen 3: Smart Integrations**
- Skip everything (select "Skip for now" for all)
- Or enable if you have Nest/Hue/Spotify
- Click "Next"

**Screen 4: Automation Limits**
- Set temperature/light/volume ranges
- Or keep the defaults
- Click "Complete Setup"

### Step 4: Final Reboot (1 minute - automatic)
- Pi saves settings and reboots
- Dashboard starts automatically
- Browser opens to: `http://localhost:8080`

### Step 5: DONE! 🎉
Your dashboard is live with:
- 🎵 Song detection
- 🔊 Sound levels
- 💡 Light levels
- 🌡️ Temperature
- 💧 Humidity
- 👥 People counting
- 😊 Comfort score
